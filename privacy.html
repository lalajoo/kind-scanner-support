Privacy policy

Kind Scanner

Documents are processed on your device and are not uploaded to a server. There are no ads or accounts, and no analytics tools tracking how you use the app. Saved PDFs stay in the app until you delete them or uninstall the app. The app-managed document folder is excluded from device backups. Unsaved edits may be lost when the app closes. Manage exported or shared copies separately. The receiving app’s privacy policy applies. Information in support emails is used only to reply. Apple App Store or Google Play handles optional purchases and purchase restoration for Submission Tools. Product lookup and purchase verification may require internet access. The app processes transaction information on your device to verify access. Documents and signature images are not sent during payment. Purchase and refund notifications: If you make an optional purchase, our notification server processes product, purchase/refund status, transaction identifier, time, and amount/currency when supplied by Apple or Google. We use Render and Google Cloud/Firestore and send a purchase summary to an operator-only Telegram chat. These notifications do not include documents, photos, signatures, payment method details, or customer names or email addresses. Raw receipts and purchase tokens are not stored in notifications or logs. The server deletes the message after successful delivery; hashed identifiers for deduplication and processing times/delivery status are retained while the service operates. Copies in Telegram may remain until deleted by the operator. This information is used only to monitor purchases and refunds, not for advertising, behavioral tracking, or linking to identities in other datasets.
